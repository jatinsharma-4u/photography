About

Hi, I’m Rohit

I’m a wedding photographer and filmmaker, and Journeys by Rohit is where I keep the stories people trust me with.

A wedding is a thousand small moments: a glance across a crowded room, a hand that won’t let go, a laugh that arrives before the vows. I don’t stage your story. I follow it. Natural light, real emotion, very little posing.
