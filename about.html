About

Hello, I'm Rohit.

A photographer and filmmaker who believes the best wedding pictures are the ones nobody posed for.

It started with a borrowed camera at a cousin's wedding. I was meant to take a few photographs. I stayed behind the lens all night, and I have been chasing that feeling ever since: the hush before the vows, a grandmother's laugh, the tired joy at the end of a long day.

Over the years the work grew from photographs to films, and from one wedding a month to celebrations in palaces, mountain valleys, beaches and city rooftops. What has stayed the same is the way we work: quietly, closely and with a lot of care.

My team and I are friendly strangers at the edge of your day. We blend in, we notice everything, and we hand you back a story that feels exactly like it did.
